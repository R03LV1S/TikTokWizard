ASSETS (BACKGROUNDS & FONTS)
====================================

This folder holds all shared assets used across your Project videos.

FOLDER BREAKDOWN:
-----------------
1. Backgrounds/
   - Videos/   -> Put your background videos here.
   - Pictures/ -> Put background images here.
   - Gifs/     -> Put animated background gifs here.
   - Overlays/ -> Put your overlays here.

2. Fonts/
   - Put your custom subtitle fonts here (.ttf, .otf).
