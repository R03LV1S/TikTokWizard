1) First create/upload a High-Quality picture as a starting point in order to create the scene you want for a video.
2) Use the prompt "USE THIS IMAGE AS THE FIRST FRAME...(now tell the ai what you want the video to do)".
3) After the video is generated, download the video file, open the video, go to the VERY last frame in the video and take a screenshot. If you are on PC use the snipping tool (i also recommend Greenshot - https://github.com/greenshot/greenshot).
4) Take the screenshot and repeat Step 1, upload the new image (preferably in a brand new chat window) and tell the ai ""USE THIS IMAGE AS THE FIRST FRAME...(now tell the ai what you want the video to do)".
5) Repeat Steps 3-4 until you have enough clips to combine and create one single video.
6) Download/run Shotcut (https://shotcut.en.softonic.com/download)
7) Once it opens ignore the "new project prompt" in the middle, drag the video files into the Playlist tab (center-left) (OR click on the 3 bars on the bottom (above "Exports", left of the Folder icon) and click "add files")
8) Drag or select all of your video files. 
9) Once all your files are in the Playlist, Drag the first video from the playlist down into the empty timeline section at the bottom of Shotcut (look at the file's timestamps and start from the earliest date/time)
10) Confirm all files end/start are aligned. (If you notice jitters or repetitions you can click on the end of the first file where the inconsistency happens (or beginning of the next file but 99% shortening the ending of the prior file works due to taking premature "last frame" screenshots) and slightly drag the length to the left and make the video shorter by the tiniest amount you can (~00:00:00.033) in order to remove any extra frames so the ending/beginning align for both videos into a smooth transition.)
11) If you want to mute the videos click on the speaker icon on the video timeline at the bottom (Output V1)
11) Once you are ready to download the finished product, click on "Export" at the center-top of the window (between Keyframes & Jobs)
12) In the new Export window (on the center-left) Set the resolution to 1920x1080 FHD (or whatever you want, FHD is the default option for tiktok/youtube/instagram/1080p)
14) Click on "Export Video/Audio" at the bottom (next to reset), select the folder you want to save the video to (TikTokWizard\START HERE\_Projects\_Assets\Backgrounds) (you should see the file generating on the right side "Jobs" window).
15) Once it's finished with a green checkmark look, Run the Wizard and select the project/settings/etc.
16) Once you are on Step 4 of the wizard, Press "B" to open the background menu and select the video you want to use.
17) Confirm/edit the settings you want and press 1 (hit enter) to render the new music video file with karaoke text from your lyrics file.

(PS. **In Shotcut you can also trim, split, add video/audio filters, reverse a video file (to create loops), mute audio (gemini/qwen like to add audio sometimes even when you prompt "silent video"), etc etc etc until you have a final product you are satisfied with.)
