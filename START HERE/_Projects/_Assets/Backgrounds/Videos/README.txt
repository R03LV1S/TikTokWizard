I would recommend you first create/upload a High-Quality picture as a base in order to create the scene you want for a video. 
Upload the picture and use it as a template for the video you want to create.
Once you have the first video, use a snipping tool (id recommend Greenshot - https://github.com/greenshot/greenshot) to take a screenshot of the last frame of the video.
Upload the last frame, and create a new video from that picture. Rinse & Repeat until you are ready to combine them.
Use Shotcut (https://shotcut.en.softonic.com/download), ignore the project prompt, drag the files into the Playlist tab (center-left), then drag those files down into the empty timeline at the bottom and arrange them in order.
You can also trim, split, add filters, reverse (to create a loop), mute audio, etc etc etc until you have a final product you are satisfied with.
