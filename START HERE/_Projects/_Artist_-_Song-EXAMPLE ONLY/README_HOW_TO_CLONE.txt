HOW TO USE THIS FOLDER TO MAKE A NEW VIDEO:
--------------------------------------------
1. COPY & PASTE THIS ENTIRE FOLDER:
   - In `_Projects/`, right-click `_Artist_Song` -> Copy (Ctrl + C).
   - Right-click in the empty space -> Paste (Ctrl + V).
   - Rename your new folder to your song's name (e.g. `Drake - Hotline Bling`).

2. ADD YOUR 2 FILES INSIDE YOUR NEW FOLDER:
   - Your Audio: Put your song file here (`song.mp3`, `.wav`, or `.m4a`).
   - Your Lyrics: Put your lyrics here (`lyrics.txt`).

3. FORMATTING YOUR LYRICS (lyrics.txt):
   - Each line in your text file appears as ONE caption line on the screen.
   - For TikTok readability, keep lines between 3 to 7 words per line.
   - Background vocals or ad-libs can be enclosed in parentheses, e.g. `(Yeah, yeah)`.

4. RUN TIKTOK WIZARD:
   - Double-click the "TikTok Wizard" shortcut on your Desktop or open `_START HERE/Windows/`.
   - Select your song from the menu.
   - Pick your resolution, font style, and hit Render!

WHAT GETS CREATED:
------------------
Your finished video will be saved right inside your project folder:
  `...\Projects\<Your Song>\<Your Song> v1.mp4`

All internal timing files (`karaoke.ass`, `.whisper_cache.json`) are neatly stored
inside `.data/` so your folder stays completely clean!
