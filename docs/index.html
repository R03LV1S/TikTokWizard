Ready In 3 Simple Steps

No video editing experience required. Run locally on your own computer in seconds.

Launch in One Click

Run the portable launcher for Windows, macOS, or Linux. Everything sets up automatically without complex installations, cloud accounts, or subscriptions.

02

Drop In Your Music

Place your audio track and lyrics into START HERE/_Projects (e.g. START HERE/_Projects/My Song), or browse and select any folder directly.

03

Choose Style & Export

Pick from 11 pre-tuned visual profiles and 8 reactive soundwaves, select full song or custom hook clip, and render a broadcast-quality MP4 in seconds.
