Ready In 3 Simple Steps

No video editing skills required. Run locally on your own computer in seconds.

01

Double-Click to Launch

Open the pre-configured folder for your OS (Windows, Mac, or Linux) and run the launcher. All dependencies and lightweight AI engines set up automatically.

02

Create Song Folder

Drop your song.mp3 and lyrics.txt into a dedicated folder inside _Projects (e.g. _Projects/My New Song).

03

Pick Style & Export

Choose full song or custom hook clip, pick from 11 visual styles and 8 soundwave visualizers, choose your canvas format, and render a broadcast-quality MP4!

8 Reactive Audio Visualizer Filters

Rendered natively in FFmpeg with hardware acceleration. No third-party plugins needed.

🌈

Constant-Q Spectrogram

High-resolution 64-frequency musical transform (showcqt) producing full-color rainbow harmonic spectral waves that pulse to every bass drop and chord change.

🔮

Circular Lissajous Orb

Phase-accurate stereo vector scope (avectorscope) mapping left and right audio channels into a circular hypnotic glowing energy ring.

〰️

Dual Stereo Oscilloscope

Mirrored split waveform monitor (showwaves) tracking the stereo spatial separation of your mix across top and bottom channels.

📊

Frequency Equalizer Bars

Discrete multi-band equalizer spectrum with frequency peak decay indicators that bounce in sync with bass, mid, and treble transients.

➖

Minimal Centered Soundwave

Ultra-clean, single-line horizontal soundwave centered across the lower canvas for an understated, modern viral aesthetic.

🚫

Clean / Background Only

Turn off visualizer overlays completely to focus 100% on your background video, anime clips, gameplay, or photo montage.
